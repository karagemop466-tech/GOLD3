Zero-hallucination policy — No Hallucinations Commitment

No hallucinations: every price, purity claim, weight and policy on this site is copied from a named official source page and stamped with the date it was read. If a source does not publish a value, the value is left blank and marked unverified — it is never estimated, inferred, or filled in to make a row look complete.

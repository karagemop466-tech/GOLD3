What "good" looks like on a listing

Green flags

  • Says solid 14k or solid 18k in the seller's own words.
  • Publishes the gram weight of the ring, not just the stone's carat weight.
  • Shows the maker's mark or names the manufacturer/assay office.
  • Return window stated in days, with the exclusions spelled out.

Red flags

  • No weight anywhere, or a weight so low the ring cannot be solid (under ~1 g for a full band).
  • “Gold” with no karat, or “14k gold plated / vermeil / filled” presented as a gold ring.
  • “Solid gold” wording on a page that also mentions a hollow centre — prohibited by 16 CFR § 23.3(c)(1).
  • Price far below the melt value of the stated weight — arithmetic that cannot be true.

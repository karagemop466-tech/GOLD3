Seller verification

A ring can be genuine and still be a bad purchase if you cannot return it after an independent test. These fields come from each seller's own policy pages, quoted and linked.

The most important line on this page. One seller's official return policy states: “Precious metals are non-refundable.” Gold jewellery is a precious metal. That directly fails the return requirement on your checklist, regardless of how good the price looks.

Verified at Costco Customer Service — What is Costco's return policy? on 22 October 2026. A second seller fails the same requirement in a different way: Tiffany & Co.'s own product page for The Tiffany® Setting states that the item is non returnable and can only be exchanged, while its general policy advertises a 30-day window.

What the reputation scores do and do not tell you

Trustpilot scores are shown with their review count and whether the business invites reviews, because those two facts change what a score means. A 4.1 from 4,875 invited reviews is a different object from a 2.7 from seven reviews on an unclaimed profile. Trustpilot itself states it does not fact-check reviews. Use the scores to find themes in the written complaints - delivery delay, warranty refusal, return-window disputes - and then ask the retailer to address that specific theme in writing before you buy.

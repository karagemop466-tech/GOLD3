What the calculator is doing

1. Fine gold

fine gold = weight × (karat ÷ 24)

14K means 14 parts gold to 10 parts alloy, so 14/24 of the metal is gold. This is the definition of karat, not an assumption about your ring.

2. Value

melt value = fine gold × spot price per gram

Spot per gram = spot per troy ounce ÷ 31.1034768. The dated spot price is loaded from the audited data file, so the arithmetic is reproducible.

3. What is excluded

Diamonds, setting labour, rhodium plating, brand and retail margin.

That is why a ring's asking price is normally several times its melt value. A low melt ratio is not fraud.

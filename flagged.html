Summary of what remains unverified

Cannot be closed by any website

  • Whether a specific ring's gold is genuinely the stated karat — needs XRF or assay on the object.
  • Whether a stamp is authentic rather than applied later.
  • Whether an estate piece has hidden repairs, solder or a replaced shank.
  • A seller's real-world reputation, service history and complaint record.

Closed by asking the seller, in writing

  • The weight in grams, and whether it excludes stones.
  • Confirmation that the item is solid gold and not hollow.
  • A photo of the stamp showing karat and maker's mark.
  • Whether the return window still applies after resizing or engraving.
  • Whether they will accept an independent test within the window.

If a seller will not let you independently verify the ring, or will not let you return it after verification, do not buy it. That is your own rule, it is the correct one, and no price justifies overriding it.

No hallucinations policy. Every flag links to the evidence that produced it, or states plainly that no source exists because the information was never published.

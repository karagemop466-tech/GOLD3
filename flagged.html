Zero-hallucination policy — No Hallucinations Commitment

No hallucinations: every price, purity claim, weight and policy on this site is copied from a named official source page and stamped with the date it was read. If a source does not publish a value, the value is left blank and marked unverified — it is never estimated, inferred, or filled in to make a row look complete. Recommendation: actively look for hallucinations by clicking the official source link on every row, re-checking live prices and weights before buying, and running our automated anti-hallucination security checks after every review pass.

F3 · None of this pass's five retailers states a hallmark or maker's-mark policy

HIGH

Under 15 U.S.C. 297, a quality mark such as '14K' must be accompanied by the manufacturer's or dealer's trademark or name. None of this pass's five retailers publishes a hallmark policy on the pages checked. A parallel pass did record hallmark observations on 3 of its 31 listings, which proves stamps can be documented when a seller photographs or states them - but the policy itself is still not promised in writing anywhere in this dataset.

This pass: 0 of 5 retailer policy pages mention a hallmark or maker's mark. Combined: 3 of 50 listings state an observed hallmark.

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/297

What to do: Before paying, ask for a close-up photograph of the inside of the band showing both the karat stamp and the maker's mark, and have the ring XRF-tested or inspected on arrival.

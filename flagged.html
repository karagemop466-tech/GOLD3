Zero-hallucination policy

Every price, purity claim, weight and policy on this site is copied from a named source page and stamped with the date it was read. If a source does not publish a value, the value is left blank and marked unverified - it is never estimated, inferred, or filled in to make a row look complete. Prices move daily: always re-check the linked page before buying.

Findings, by severity

F1 · Blue Nile's 18K ring page contradicts itself on the metal

HIGH

The title and specification block of the Floral Marquise ring say '18K White Gold' / 'Metal: 18K White Gold'. The Product Details paragraph on the very same page says the ring is 'artfully crafted from warmly lustrous 14k yellow gold'. Both statements cannot be true. This is the clearest proof in the dataset that a karat claim on a product page is not verification.

Title: 'Floral Marquise Diamond Engagement Ring in 18k White Gold' · Spec: 'Metal 18K White Gold' · Product Details: 'artfully crafted from warmly lustrous 14k yellow gold'

Source: https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/floral-marquise-diamond-engagement-ring-in-18k-white-gold-item-203738

See the listing in the table →

What to do: Ask Blue Nile in writing which metal the ring is made from before ordering, and confirm the karat on the invoice.

F2 · Only 4 of 38 listings publish a weight - none from this pass's five retailers

HIGH

Within this pass, 0 of 17 gold listings state the finished ring weight in grams. Across the combined dataset (38 listings, 10 sellers) the figure rises to 4, and even those are not directly usable: two are Ross-Simons bands whose figures the seller labels approximate, and two are Wilson's Estate Jewelry rings whose own weight quote says the number is the total item weight including the diamond. So no listing in the combined dataset publishes a clean gold-only weight. Aurate's product feed does carry an undocumented numeric 'weight' field, but it repeats across variants and is never described as the finished item weight, so it was deliberately not used.

This pass: 0 of 17 listings state a weight. Combined: 4 of 38 - two flagged approximate by the seller, two stated as total weight including the stone.

Source: https://auratenewyork.com/products/2ct-round-lab-grown-diamond-solitaire-ring.js

What to do: Ask the seller in writing for the gold-only weight in grams before paying, and keep the reply. Never substitute a total item weight (which includes the stone) for a gold weight - the difference can be a large share of the metal.

F3 · None of this pass's five retailers states a hallmark or maker's-mark policy

HIGH

Under 15 U.S.C. 297, a quality mark such as '14K' must be accompanied by the manufacturer's or dealer's trademark or name. None of this pass's five retailers publishes a hallmark policy on the pages checked. A parallel pass did record hallmark observations on 3 of its 19 listings, which proves stamps can be documented when a seller photographs or states them - but the policy itself is still not promised in writing anywhere in this dataset.

This pass: 0 of 5 retailer policy pages mention a hallmark or maker's mark. Combined: 3 of 38 listings state an observed hallmark.

Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/297

What to do: Before paying, ask for a close-up photograph of the inside of the band showing both the karat stamp and the maker's mark, and have the ring XRF-tested or inspected on arrival.

F4 · 7 of 17 listings never use the word 'solid'

MEDIUM

The checklist requires the listing itself to say solid gold. 7 of the 17 solid-gold listings (five Blue Nile settings and two Ring Concierge rings) state a karat but never use the word 'solid'. Seven more rely on a brand-level policy rather than the listing. Mejuri states 'solid gold' in both of its listings, and Catbird carries 'Solid' in the product name.

Listings with solid_gold = null: 7. Listings with solid_gold = true stated only at brand level: 7. Listings stating solid gold on the listing: 3.

Source: https://www.bluenile.com/engagement-rings/design-your-own-ring/petite-solitaire-engagement-ring-in-14k-yellow-gold-item-195639

What to do: Get the words 'solid 14k gold' (or 18k) into the order confirmation or an email before paying.

Verification passes

The build refuses to run unless this pass is clean, so an unsourced or contradictory value cannot reach the published site. Run the pass again after every review or price recapture - and specifically look for hallucinations: a number that does not appear in its own quote, a weight nobody published, or a policy that has drifted.

  1. Every populated factual field carries a source URL on a recognised official domain (https only).
  2. Every price has a currency, a capture date, a source page and a verbatim quote.
  3. The recorded price must literally appear inside that quote - the transcription / hallucination check.
  4. Unpublished values stay null (weight, carat, hallmark); a value without a source is an error.
  5. Purity matches the category: a 14K page cannot contain an 18K ring.
  6. Plated / vermeil / silver items can only exist in the excluded category.
  7. A solid-gold claim without a verbatim retailer excerpt is an error.
  8. Price-vs-melt sanity: a price below the melt value of 0.5 g of its own karat is rejected as impossible.
  9. Prices older than 30 days are flagged stale.
  10. Two independent gold spot sources must agree within 2%.
  11. Findings must have a severity, an official source, an action, and only known placeholders.
  12. Metric arithmetic must reconcile: gold + excluded = total, and status buckets must sum.

How a flag gets closed

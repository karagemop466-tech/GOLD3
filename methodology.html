Method and verification log

Line by line, this is how a number earns a place in the table — and what happens to the numbers that cannot.

The rule that governs everything here

If it cannot be quoted from an official page, it does not go in the table. No exceptions for “everyone knows” facts, no rounding to look tidy, no filling a gap because the page looked trustworthy. A blank cell is an honest result; a plausible-looking invention is not.

Passes run, in order

#PassWhat it doesOutcome
0Scope Read the repo, the coordination board and the checklist before claiming anything. Repo held only a stub README at one commit. A second session had in fact started at the same moment without appearing on the board — see pass 4b and 6.
1Primary-source fetch Open each seller's and regulator's own page; capture exact wording, not summaries. 8 sellers, 19 listings, 8 legal rules, 1 benchmark.
2Quote matching Attach the verbatim sentence to each price, weight and purity claim. Every populated value carries a quote.
3Blank discipline Any field absent from the official page is set to null and flagged. Weight published on only a minority of rows; flagged, not estimated.
4Self-audit Run scripts/audit.py: it re-derives arithmetic and hunts invented values. Caught three of this project's own overreaches; all corrected before publishing.
4bParallel-session reconciliation Merge the second session's dataset, re-open each of its unique listings, keep its facts and record what was superseded. 5 of its 6 live rows re-verified by this session and folded in; 2 dead listings moved to “excluded”; its original files archived, not deleted.
5Link and build check Resolve every local link, verify external sources are https, confirm the site builds. Clean.
6Deployed-page read-back Fetch the published GitHub Pages URL after the merge and compare what the page says against the rows in the dataset. First attempt failed: the repository had leftover page templates whose baked-in counts contradicted the 19-row dataset. Fixed and re-verified — the live page and the data now agree.

Pass 6 is not optional. Every local check passed while the deployed site was still wrong, because the defect lived in the gap between the repository's pages and its data. A project about not misleading readers has to read back the thing readers actually see. That is now the last step before any release is called done.

The audit earned its keep immediately. On its first run it rejected three estate rows where this project had marked a ring as “solid” on the strength of a stamp stating the karat. The sellers never used the word “solid”. The verdicts were downgraded to unverified and flagged S2. That is precisely the inference your checklist warns against, and it was caught by machine rather than by luck.

Why some obvious sources are absent

  • No LBMA Gold Price. Its historical tables require a licence; quoting a figure would mean reproducing data this project cannot verify.
  • No independent review scores. None was verified against a primary review source, so no seller is rated. Flagged M1.
  • No “average retail premium” figure. No official source publishes one for engagement rings; any percentage would be invented.
  • No maker's mark where the seller did not state one. Absence is recorded as absence.

Reproducing the checks

From the repository root:

python3 scripts/audit.py

The script parses every JSON file and fails when:

  • a price does not appear in its own quoted source text;
  • a gram figure is not present in the quote that is supposed to support it;
  • a “pass” on solid gold exists without the seller saying “solid”;
  • a flag is referenced but never defined;
  • a field is both verified and unverified;
  • a phone or email appears without a contact source;
  • a karat fraction does not equal n ÷ 24;
  • a local link is broken, or the HTML prints a figure that exists nowhere in the data.

Its output is committed at reports/audit-report.md so the state of the dataset is inspectable, not just asserted.

Standing recommendation. Re-run the audit after every review, then do one deliberate human pass whose only job is to look for hallucinations: pick any number on the site and try to find it on the linked page. If it is not there, delete it and mark the field unverified. Better an empty cell than a confident error.

Collaboration, conflict and change control

Two sessions worked this repository in parallel. A second session merged its own implementation to main (PR #1) while this one was still building, so the merge conflicted. Neither side was thrown away: the second session's seven verified listings were re-opened, re-verified and folded into the dataset; its seller facts were folded into the register; its two dead listings moved to the excluded table; and its original pages, data, scripts and reports are preserved verbatim under archive/session-982bfc88/. Its independently captured spot price matched this session's figure exactly — a genuine two-session cross-check of the benchmark. The reconciliation is logged in COORDINATION.md.

COORDINATION.md in the repository root is the shared task board: task, status, owning session, and the files it may touch. Claims are committed before work starts, scopes do not overlap, and shared outputs are only changed by the integration step. The full claim and hand-off history is kept there rather than rewritten.

No hallucinations policy. This page documents the verification, including the failures it caught. Nothing here is asserted without a source.

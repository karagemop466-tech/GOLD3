Zero-hallucination policy — No Hallucinations Commitment

No hallucinations: every price, purity claim, weight and policy on this site is copied from a named official source page and stamped with the date it was read. If a source does not publish a value, the value is left blank and marked unverified — it is never estimated, inferred, or filled in to make a row look complete. Recommendation: actively look for hallucinations by clicking the official source link on every row, re-checking live prices and weights before buying, and running our automated anti-hallucination security checks after every review pass.

Where the two passes agree - and where they don't

Agree: the gold spot anchor. Dataset B records the World Gold Council figure of $4,024.36/oz (21 Oct 2026); Dataset A uses GoldPrice.org at $4,026.79/oz (22 Oct 2026) and lists the World Gold Council figure as its cross-check. The two are 0.06% apart.

Agree: "solid gold" is rarely stated on listings. Both passes independently flagged it as the norm rather than the exception.

Differ: weight publication. Dataset A's five retailers publish none; Dataset B found Ross-Simons publishing approximate gram weights. That is why finding F2 is now scoped to "this pass" and reports the combined figure separately.

Differ: coverage. Dataset B includes Tiffany, Costco, James Allen, Ross-Simons and Wilson’s Estate Jewelry, which Dataset A did not open; Dataset A includes Aurate and Ring Concierge, which Dataset B did not.

Differ: hallmark and weight reporting. Dataset B records hallmark/maker’s-mark observations and four published weights (two of them total item weights that include the stone); Dataset A records none. Both are correct about their own sources — which is exactly why the columns are kept apart and labelled.

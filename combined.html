Zero-hallucination policy

Every price, purity claim, weight and policy on this site is copied from a named source page and stamped with the date it was read. If a source does not publish a value, the value is left blank and marked unverified - it is never estimated, inferred, or filled in to make a row look complete. Prices move daily: always re-check the linked page before buying.

Where the two passes agree - and where they don't

Agree: the gold spot anchor. Dataset B records the World Gold Council figure of $4,024.36/oz (21 Oct 2026); Dataset A uses GoldPrice.org at $4,026.79/oz (22 Oct 2026) and lists the World Gold Council figure as its cross-check. The two are 0.06% apart.

Agree: "solid gold" is rarely stated on listings. Both passes independently flagged it as the norm rather than the exception.

Differ: weight publication. Dataset A's five retailers publish none; Dataset B found Ross-Simons publishing approximate gram weights. That is why finding F2 is now scoped to "this pass" and reports the combined figure separately.

Differ: coverage. Dataset B includes Tiffany, Costco, James Allen and Ross-Simons, which Dataset A did not open; Dataset A includes Aurate and Ring Concierge, which Dataset B did not.

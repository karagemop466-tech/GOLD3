Important: read the FTC's “solid” example in context

16 CFR § 23.3 specifically discusses hollow-center gold-alloy products: the hollow center should be disclosed, and such products should not be described as solid. This is not a blanket statement that every 14K alloy ring is hollow. The product pages here still do not explicitly say “solid” or rule out coatings. Read the eCFR text · Official published CFR PDF.

Independent verification sequence

Do not wear or resize until the test and return process is settled.

BEFORE PURCHASE

Get answers in writing

Gold-only grams, hollow/coated construction, exact marks, and whether a third-party inspection preserves the return right.

ON ARRIVAL

Keep the ring unworn

Photograph the package, product, stamp and maker mark. Retain the listing, receipt, diamond paperwork, and all seller messages.

INDEPENDENT TEST

Ask a professional

Request XRF or another appropriate purity test from an independent jeweler or lab. Ask about surface plating and mixed components; the method has limits.

Before you consider either option

Keep the decision reversible until the evidence is complete.

  1. Ask for the exact ring's gold-only and total weight in grams; do not infer it from carat weight or photos.
  2. Ask for a close photo of the physical karat stamp and maker's mark, plus a written statement about coatings and hollow construction.
  3. Confirm in writing that a third-party jeweler's inspection/XRF is allowed and will not remove your return right.
  4. Re-open the listing at checkout and save the final item, size, price, policy, and seller correspondence.

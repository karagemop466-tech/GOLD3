Why nothing is cleared

Missing evidence is not replaced with a guess.

  • None of the three listings gives a gold-only weight in grams.
  • None documents the physical karat stamp and maker's mark.
  • None explicitly states solid gold or excludes plating in the captured copy.
  • Return terms do not say whether independent XRF testing preserves eligibility.
  • No current gold quote is embedded; metal value stays blank.
!

Read “solid” precisely

FTC guidance addresses disclosure for hollow-center gold-alloy pieces and says such items should not be described as solid. This is not a blanket claim that every 14K ring is hollow. Read 16 CFR § 23.3.

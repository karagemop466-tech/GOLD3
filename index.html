None of this pass's five retailers states a hallmark or maker's-mark policy

HIGH

A parallel pass did record hallmark observations on 3 of its 31 listings, which proves stamps can be documented when a seller photographs or states them - but the policy itself is still not promised in writing anywhere in this dataset.

Why this exists

A stamp is a claim, not proof

Under 15 U.S.C. 295 a gold mark is allowed a tolerance; under 15 U.S.C. 297 it must also carry a maker's mark. Neither makes the metal real - only an assay does.

"Solid" is a regulated word

16 CFR 23.3(c)(1) says a hollow ring must not be described as solid gold. If a listing will not say the word, get it in writing before you pay.

Weight is the missing number

Not one of the 17 gold listings publishes the finished ring weight. Without it you cannot know how much gold you are buying - you can only see what the price implies.

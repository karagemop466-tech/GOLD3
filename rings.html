What is missing from these listings, and why it is blank

Weight

Brand-new rings from large retailers almost never state a metal weight in grams. Without a weight, a price-per-gram test is impossible, so those cells stay empty and carry flag W1.

Estate jewellers do publish grams — and also publish the stamp and maker's mark — which is why they dominate the rows that can actually be analysed.

Hallmark and maker's mark

Online listings rarely photograph the inside of the band. A karat claim with no maker's mark is incomplete under the National Stamping Act, so it is flagged H1 rather than assumed.

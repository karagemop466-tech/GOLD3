A 2 mm 18K band listed at 0.7 g. That is a genuinely thin band — the seller qualifies it with "All weights and measurements are approximate". Kept in the dataset on purpose, as the counter-example. "18k gold vermeil" describes a 2.5-micron gold layer over sterling silver — the same threshold 16 CFR § 23.4 sets for the word vermeil.

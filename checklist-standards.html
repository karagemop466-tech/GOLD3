The 12 Mandatory Buyer Verification Rules

Each rule links directly to an official statutory, regulatory, or live empirical primary source.

Rule 01

Check Karat Purity Explicitly (10K / 14K / 18K / 22K / 24K)

Confirm exact fractional purity: 10K (41.7% — US legal minimum under 16 CFR § 23.3), 14K (58.3% — classic durable daily bridal balance), 18K (75.0% — richer yellow hue, slightly softer), 22K (91.7%), or 24K (99.95% pure bullion softness).

16 CFR Part 23 (FTC Jewelry Guides) ↗
Rule 02

Verify Explicit 'Solid Gold' Wording

Require unambiguous 'Solid 14K Gold' or 'Solid 18K Gold' wording. Reject 'Gold Plated', 'Gold Vermeil' (gold over sterling silver), 'Gold Filled' (mechanically bonded layer), or 'Gold over Brass' (see SOL-09 control at $29).

16 CFR § 23.3 Misrepresentation of Gold Content ↗
Rule 03

Confirm Mandatory Karat & Maker's Trademark Hallmarks

Under the US National Gold and Silver Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. § 297), any quality mark ('14K', '585', '750') MUST be accompanied by the registered manufacturer trademark. UK items >1.0g require statutory Assay Office hallmarks.

15 U.S.C. Ch. 8 Stamping Act ↗
Rule 04

Ask for Exact Gram Weight Before Paying

Millimeter width (e.g., 1.8mm vs 4.0mm) tells you dimensions, not hollow vs solid density. Request net gold weight in grams via live chat/email whenever omitted (Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, Mejuri).

NIST Weight Standards ↗
Rule 05

Check Independent Seller Reputation, Address & Phone

Verify physical headquarters, working toll-free phone, and independent BBB complaint history — never rely solely on curated on-site reviews (e.g., Mejuri holds a BBB D- rating despite glossy site reviews).

Rule 06

Compare Asking Price Against Live Raw Gold Melt Value

At $4,025.10/oz ($129.41/g 24K), pure 14K gold melts at $75.49/g and 18K at $97.06/g. Any 'solid gold' ring priced near or below raw melt value is counterfeit, hollow ultra-thin foil, or plated brass.

Rule 07

Read Return Policy Exclusions (Resized, Engraved & Custom)

Crucial trap: Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth void returns once a ring is engraved or resized! Menē gives only 7 calendar days from delivery. Never engrave or resize before independent verification.

Rule 08

Pay Strictly by Major Credit Card for Dispute Protection

Never pay via Zelle, crypto, cash app, or bank wire — even when tempted by Blue Nile's or Brilliant Earth's 1.5% wire discount — so you preserve statutory Fair Credit Billing Act chargeback protection.

Rule 09

Demand Detailed Written Invoice & Grading Certificates

Require an itemized invoice specifying metal karat, gram weight, ring size, SKU, and natural/lab stone status, plus verifiable GIA report numbers (e.g., GIA #2225975086 on SOL-07) or Menē CoA.

Rule 10

Inspect Immediately at an Independent Local Jeweler

On Day 1–2 of arrival (with security tags intact!), have an independent bench jeweler inspect prongs, weigh the ring on a legal-for-trade 0.01g scale, and verify internal karat + maker stamps.

Rule 11

Verify Alloy Purity Non-Destructively via XRF Analyzer

Ask a local coin dealer, refiner, or independent jeweler to run a 30-second non-destructive X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) scan — never acid-scratch a returnable ring, as damage voids return rights.

Walk Away Whenever Answers Are Vague or Contradictory

If a listing mixes '10kt' and '14kt' on the same page (SOL-08), contradicts its own resizing banner (SOL-05), refuses to state solid gold purity, or pressures instant wire payment — walk away.

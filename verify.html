Zero-hallucination policy — No Hallucinations Commitment

No hallucinations: every price, purity claim, weight and policy on this site is copied from a named official source page and stamped with the date it was read. If a source does not publish a value, the value is left blank and marked unverified — it is never estimated, inferred, or filled in to make a row look complete. What no website can verify

Needs the object in hand

  • true karat of the actual object (needs XRF/assay)
  • authenticity of a stamp
  • hidden repairs on estate pieces
  • seller complaint history

How to close that gap yourself

Take the ring, before wearing or resizing it, to an independent jeweller — not the seller — and ask for an XRF test or an assay, plus a weight on a calibrated scale. Compare the reading with the karat stamp and with the weight recorded here, and keep the test receipt with your order documentation.

If the reading is below the stamp, stop: contact the seller in writing inside the return window and keep the ring unworn.

If a seller will not let you independently verify the ring, or will not let you return it after verification, do not buy it. No price and no brand reputation overrides that rule.

Standing recommendation

Re-run the audit after every review, then spend one pass doing nothing but hunting hallucinations: take any number on this site and try to find it on the linked page. If it is not there, delete it and mark the field unverified. This page is that pass, written down.
